## Story

As an Activity Manager, I want visual status and role badges displayed alongside user profiles so that team rosters and member states can be scanned quickly.

## Acceptance criteria

- Highlights inactive accounts and distinct team roles across member directories and management views.

## Progress

A manager is a sky badge with a star, and a member is a gray badge with a person icon, on the team member list and on each team membership. The inactive badge for a suspended account is still open. The old global role is not a separate badge.
