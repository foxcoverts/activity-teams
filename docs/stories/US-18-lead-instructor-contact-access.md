## Story

As a Lead Instructor, I want time-bound access to participant contact and emergency details relative to my scheduled booking window so I can manage coordination and handle emergencies safely.

## Acceptance criteria

- Contact and emergency details become available from 00:00 on the booking start date through 23:59:59 on the booking end date.
- Viewing data requires clicking an explicit GDPR consent confirmation checkbox acknowledging strict use for team matters.

## Progress

A manager of the team can view their members' phone and emergency details outside the booking window. A system admin does not bypass this guard. Confirm which timezone determines the booking dates when implementing the boundary.
