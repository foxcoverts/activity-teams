## Story

As a lead instructor, I want to see the qualifications and keys of the people on a booking I lead, including Scout permit restrictions, so I can decide how to deploy the team on the day.

## Acceptance criteria

- On a booking they lead, the lead can see the people on that session.
- Each person shows whether they meet the activity. Opening that shows the in-date qualifications that meet the activity, and the lead can open a qualification's detail, including Scout permit restrictions. They can also ask to see that person's other qualifications.
- Each person shows whether they hold a key this booking needs, or hold other keys. Opening that shows the rings they hold. The lead can also see the keys the booking needs and every ring that opens them, including a ring held by someone who is not on the session.
- A member who is not the lead on that booking cannot see those qualifications, restrictions, or key rings.
- Phone numbers and emergency contacts stay out of this view.
- Any other personal details about someone on the roster are shown only after the lead instructor acknowledges their proper use as required by [US-58](US-58-personal-data-viewing-acknowledgement.md).

## Progress

On the booking they lead, the lead sees the people on the session. Each person shows whether they meet the activity and whether they hold a key the session needs. Opening a badge shows the in-date qualifications that meet the activity, with a way to see the others and a permit's restrictions, or the rings that person holds. Keys on the booking lists every ring that opens a required key, including one held by someone who is not on the session. Another member on that booking does not see the roster or those keys. [US-18](US-18-lead-instructor-contact-access.md) remains the story for a lead's access to phone numbers and emergency contacts.
