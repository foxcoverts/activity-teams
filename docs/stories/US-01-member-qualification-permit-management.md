## Story

As an Activity Manager, I want to record and maintain team members' qualifications (supporting Scout permits, Mountain Training awards, and historical Girlguiding schemes) so that I can easily verify authorisation to supervise specific activities.

## Acceptance criteria

- Supports polymorphic qualification types with distinct tracking rules (Scout permit expirations, non-expiring Mountain Training, and historical Girlguiding).
- Managers can create, update, or remove qualifications for active members.
