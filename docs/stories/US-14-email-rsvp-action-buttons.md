## Story

As a Team Member, I want secure, one-click action links inside invitation emails to update my attendance instantly without logging into the full system.

## Acceptance criteria

- Email notifications include action links for Yes, Maybe, and No.
- Links authorise only that single action without granting broader app access.
- Engineered with pre-fetch defences against automated email scanner bots.
