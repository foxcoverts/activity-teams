## Story

As a Lead Instructor, I want time-bound access to participant contact and emergency details relative to my scheduled booking window so I can manage coordination and handle emergencies safely.

## Acceptance criteria

- Contact details become visible strictly relative to the event window (beforehand and retained briefly afterward).
- Viewing data requires clicking an explicit GDPR consent confirmation checkbox acknowledging strict use for team matters.

## Progress

A lead instructor's time-bound access stays as written. A manager of the team can view their members' phone and emergency details outside that booking window. A system admin does not bypass this guard. The time window and consent checkbox are not built yet.
