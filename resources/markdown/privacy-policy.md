# Privacy Notice

**This privacy notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our invitation-only team booking and management application.**

As the **Data Controller**, Hinckley and District Scout Council ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018).

This platform is strictly an internal administrative tool used to run invitation-only activity, climbing, and warden teams. It does not open self-registration to the public, nor does it process personal data of visiting external hire groups beyond recording the organization or group name (e.g., for calendar scheduling).

### What We Do Not Do
* We **never** open registration to the public; access is strictly by invitation.
* We **never** sell or rent your personal data to third parties.
* We **never** use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.
* We **never** collect dates of birth or participant ages on bookings.

## 2. The Data We Collect About You

We only collect and process personal data that is strictly necessary to run our campsite activity teams safely and securely.

* **Identity Data:** Full name, manager-set under-18 safeguarding flag, and timezone.
* **Account Data:** Email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), and email verification status.
* **Contact Data:** Your phone number, plus the name and phone number of your designated emergency contact.
* **Team & Operational Data:** Team memberships, roles, adventurous activity permits/qualifications (e.g., Climbing Tower, Archery, Air Rifles), booking attendance, and lead-instructor history.
* **Application Records:** Privacy notice acceptance timestamp, annual contact-confirmation timestamp, secure calendar-feed tokens, and activity/system audit logs.
* **Viewing Acknowledgements:** A permanent record of who viewed another person's contact or emergency details, capturing the viewer's identity, the viewed party's section, and the exact timestamp (with names frozen at that moment).
* **Placeholder Data:** For individuals who are invited but have not yet registered, we hold their name, under-18 safeguarding flag, invitation email, and intended team role.

## 4. Children’s Data

Because managers can mark accounts as under 18, **we process the data of individuals under the age of 18**.
* **Operational Control:** Accounts are created strictly via closed invitations managed by adult team managers.
* **Minimisation:** We do not ask for or store dates of birth or ages within booking records.
* **Extended Retention:** Because minors can legally bring personal injury claims up until their 21st birthday, we retain basic attendance history and names indefinitely to defend against future legal claims.

## 5. Who Can See Your Data (Data Sharing and Access Rules)

We enforce strict, time-locked access controls within the application to ensure your information is only visible to those who strictly need it.

* **The Individual:** You can always see and edit your own profile (name, email, phone, emergency contact, timezone). Nobody else can see or set your password.
* **Team Managers:** Managers can view and edit the profile details of members in their teams.
* **Phone Numbers:** Visible to the lead instructor and other members listed on the exact same booking. This visibility is strictly time-bound: it starts at the booking start time and vanishes at the booking end time (based on the booking's timezone).
* **Emergency Contacts:** Visible to fellow participants on the same booking *only* during the active booking window (from start date/time through end date/time), ensuring help can be reached if you are incapacitated. Team managers can access these details outside of booking windows for ongoing welfare and safety management.
* **The Acknowledgement Wall:** Before any user can view another person's phone or emergency details, they must actively tick a box certifying they will use the information only for legitimate team matters. This acknowledgement expires after 30 minutes, is tracked per person and per section, and does not override standard access rules.
* **Qualifications:** Award levels and expiry dates (e.g., climbing permits) are open operational records and are not treated under restrictive contact data rules.
* **Visibility of Departed Members:** Regular members can never see deleted or departed individuals. Team managers will see a named historical shell (e.g., in past attendance sheets or as historical lead instructors).

## 6. Where Your Data is Hosted (Data Processors)

To maintain data sovereignty, your data is tightly controlled within the UK:
* **Application Hosting:** The application and its database are hosted within the United Kingdom using **IONOS** as our infrastructure sub-processor.
* **Email Infrastructure:** Outbound invitations, password resets, and operational alerts are sent via email. Our email processing provider is **ForwardEmail**.
* **Avatars:** Profile photos are loaded from **Gravatar** using a hash of your email address. You can change the photo on Gravatar. Gravatar is operated by Automattic.
* **International Transfers:** All core application and database data remains hosted within the UK. Avatar requests go to Gravatar.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

* **Active Accounts:** We keep your full profile and account details for as long as you remain an active member or warden of the platform.
* **The 30-Day Deletion Process:** When an account is flagged for deletion (either by yourself using your password, or by an oversight administrator), login access is blocked immediately. For **30 days**, the data remains in a restorable state so a System Administrator can undo an accidental deletion.
* **The Hard Purge:** After exactly 30 days, a hard purge occurs. The system permanently wipes your email, phone number, emergency contact details, password hash, remember tokens, calendar tokens, timezone, access flags, and confirmation timestamps.
* **The Permanent Named Shell:** Following the purge, your `ID`, `Name`, `Section`, `Qualifications`, `Past Attendance History`, and `Lead Instructor History` are kept **indefinitely** as a named shell. This is structurally required because limitation periods for injuries can extend to 3 years for adults, or until age 21 for minors. Because we do not collect ages, we cannot safely automate an automated expiry cutoff.
* **Audit Logs and Acknowledgements:** Viewing-acknowledgement trail logs are kept for 6 months and are then automatically pruned. These logs are not wiped when an individual account is deleted, preventing an individual from abusing access privileges and erasing their footprint by deleting their account.
* **System Safeguards:** The last remaining System Administrator cannot delete themselves. The last manager of a team can delete their account; when this happens, System Administrators are notified via email. No confirmation email is dispatched to the user post-deletion.

## 8. Your Legal Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the following rights which you can exercise by contacting us:

* **Right of Access & Portability:** You can instantly download an in-app personal data export in standard JSON format. This export contains your profile, memberships, qualifications, and attendance logs. To protect the privacy of third parties, it does not include other people's contact or emergency information.
* **Right to Rectification:** You can directly update your profile details and complete your annual contact confirmation in the app at any time.
* **Right to Restriction / Objection:** You have the right to object to or restrict certain types of processing where we rely on Legitimate Interests.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You can request the deletion of your account. Please note that your right to erasure is limited by our overriding legitimate interest to defend against legal claims, meaning your historical named shell will remain intact as detailed in Section 7.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint at any time with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection issues (www.ico.org.uk).
