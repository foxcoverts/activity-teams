## Story

As an Activity Manager, I want to find a person who is not a current member of a booking's team and invite them onto that booking so that help from outside the team can be asked without a guest role.

## Acceptance criteria

- A manager can find a person who is not a current member of the booking's team and invite them onto that booking.
- Inviting them does not give them a guest role or a membership of that team.

## Progress

Not built yet. The invite list is the booking's current team. The old global guest role is not this story.
