## Story

As an Activity Manager, I want to track and record merchandise and equipment issuances — including complimentary items like neckers given to members — so that I have a clear historical record to verify replacement eligibility or manage chargeable requests.

## Acceptance criteria

- Maintains an allocation history log for items (such as group neckers) against individual member profiles, recording issue dates and quantities.
- Allows managers to review previous complimentary issuances when members request replacement gear.
