## Story

As an instructor or team member with registered personal kit, I want to receive automated warnings before my equipment accreditations expire so that I can arrange re-inspection proactively.

## Acceptance criteria

- Automatically dispatches reminder notifications ahead of registered kit inspection expiration dates.
- Surfaces equipment expiry alerts on individual profile dashboards and notification logs.
