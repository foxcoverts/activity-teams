## Story

As a team member or activity leader, I want to submit incident and accident reports through the platform so that safety compliance records are properly maintained, relevant managers are notified immediately, and I receive a confirmation copy for my records.

## Acceptance criteria

- Provides a structured incident reporting form to capture necessary details like date, description, and involved parties.
- Requires the reporter to select the responsible team and capture the relevant activity or venue associated with the incident.
- If the reporter belongs to only one team, that team is selected by default; otherwise, the reporter selects the responsible team.
- Automatically notifies the manager or managers of the selected team.
- Automatically sends a copy or confirmation of the submitted report to the original reporter for their records.
- Only the reporter and managers of the selected team can access the report. Before personal details about another person in the report are displayed, the viewer must acknowledge their proper use as required by [US-58](US-58-personal-data-viewing-acknowledgement.md).

## Progress

There is no activity manager or venue manager, because activities and venues are shared. The reporter selects the responsible team; report editability after submission and retention still need refinement.
