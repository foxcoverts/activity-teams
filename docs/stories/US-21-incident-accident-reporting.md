## Story

As a team member or activity leader, I want to submit incident and accident reports through the platform so that safety compliance records are properly maintained, relevant managers are notified immediately, and I receive a confirmation copy for my records.

## Acceptance criteria

- Provides a structured incident reporting form to capture necessary details like date, description, and involved parties.
- Requires the reporter to select the relevant activity or venue associated with the incident.
- Automatically triggers notifications to the specific manager responsible for that selected activity or venue.
- Automatically sends a copy or confirmation of the submitted report to the original reporter for their records.
