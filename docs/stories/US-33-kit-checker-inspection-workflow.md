## Story

As a designated Kit Checker, I want to record and verify annual safety inspections for instructors' personal specialist equipment so that equipment compliance is formally logged in the system.

## Acceptance criteria

- Allows users with the designated Kit Checker role to record, update, and sign off on equipment safety inspection dates for instructors.
- Maintains a clear audit trail of who performed the kit check and when it was verified.
