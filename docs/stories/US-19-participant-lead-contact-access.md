## Story

As a team member participating in an event, I want to access the lead instructor's contact details on the day of the booking (to report running late) and view their emergency contacts if an incident occurs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Members on a confirmed booking roster can view the lead instructor's phone details on the event day.
- Emergency access requires explicit GDPR consent acknowledgment.

## Progress

A system admin does not bypass these GDPR controls. Not built yet.
