## Story

As an Activity Manager, I want to record and maintain team members' qualifications (Scout permits, Mountain Training awards, and Girlguiding awards) so that I can easily verify authorisation to supervise specific activities.

## Acceptance criteria

- Supports qualification types with distinct tracking rules. Scout permits and Girlguiding awards expire. Girlguiding is a closed set: a manager can record an award someone already holds, and once it has expired no replacement is issued. Mountain Training is the only type that does not expire.
- Managers can create, update, or remove qualifications for active members.

## Progress

A manager of the current team can add, edit, and remove qualifications for that team's current members. The records stay on the person, so a manager of another current team edits the same rows. Oversight can read them and cannot change them. The member can read their own list from Qualifications in the team menu. Changes are logged.
