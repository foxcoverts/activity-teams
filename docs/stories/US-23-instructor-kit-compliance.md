## Story

As an Activity Manager, I want to track and record annual safety inspections for instructors' personal specialist equipment (such as harnesses and helmets) so that we mitigate risk and ensure compliance before instructors lead safety-critical sessions.

## Acceptance criteria

- Allows managers to view equipment safety inspection and accreditation statuses for all instructors.
- Flags expired or unverified instructor kit statuses on management views to prevent non-compliant instructors from leading sessions.
