## Story

As an Activity Manager, I want different types of bookings to enforce or bypass specific qualification requirements (for example, Scout climbing versus general maintenance) so that staffing validation matches the activity type.

## Acceptance criteria

- Bookings link to specific activity governance types.
- Lead instructor and attendee validation dynamically adjust based on the activity profile.

## Progress

The recorded qualification fields, including cover on the scheme enums, are the ones an activity's alternatives will match. An activity does not require a qualification yet, and the booking confirm check is still open.
