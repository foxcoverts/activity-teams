## Story

As an invited team member, I want to manage my account security and exercise control over my personal data, so that I can keep my account secure and leave the service with confidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- Account creation remains invitation-only; this story does not add public registration.
- An authenticated member can change their password using the account's security requirements and can use the existing secure password-reset flow if they cannot sign in.
- A member can request and obtain a copy of the personal data held about them in a usable, commonly readable format.
- A member can initiate account deletion through a clearly explained, confirmed process that verifies the request and prevents accidental deletion.
- Once deletion is completed, the member can no longer sign in and their personal data is erased or anonymised unless there is a documented legal or operational reason to retain specific information.
- Any retained information is limited to what is necessary, access-controlled, and kept only for a defined retention period; shared operational records are handled without unnecessarily exposing the former member's personal data.
- The member is informed of the outcome and any information that must be retained, with the reason and retention period.

## Refinement

Confirm the data included in the export, request verification and completion workflow, retention exceptions and periods, and treatment of shared booking, incident, audit, and legal records. This story supports privacy rights but is not by itself a guarantee of GDPR compliance; the controller's lawful bases, privacy information, and retention policy must also be confirmed.
