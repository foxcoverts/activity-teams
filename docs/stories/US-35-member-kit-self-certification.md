## Story

As an instructor or team member, I want to self-certify the inspection of my own personal specialist kit so that I can maintain active compliance without bottlenecking on designated Kit Checkers.

## Acceptance criteria

- Provides an accessible self-certification option in user profile or kit management views to log personal equipment checks.
- Records the self-certification date and notes with a distinct audit label, ensuring members can clear their own routine compliance blocks while managers and checkers retain visibility.
